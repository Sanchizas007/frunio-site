Know what to eat first.

Frunio keeps every product in your kitchen with its use-by date. It tells you what is about to expire, so you cook it before it goes in the bin, and it suggests a dinner from what you already have.

What Frunio does

THREE WAYS TO ADD FOOD

Scan a barcode, photograph a receipt and add the whole shop at once, or type a product in by hand. Market produce has no barcode: pick the product and the day you bought it, and Frunio estimates how long it keeps. Shelf life comes from a food-safety table, and you can correct any date.

EAT FIRST

The home screen and a widget show what to use soon, a Lock Screen card appears on the day something expires, and one reminder a day replaces a pile of alerts.

ONE KITCHEN, WHOLE FAMILY

Invite your family with a link. Everyone sees the same fridge and pantry and updates it from their own iPhone. There is no account to create and no server of ours in between: it syncs through your own iCloud.

SMALL THINGS THAT HELP

  • Dinner ideas from what you have, made on your iPhone: nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Receipts are read on your phone and never leave it.
  • Fridge, freezer and pantry, with an opened-package date.
  • 20 languages, each with its own local recipes.

A look inside

TRY IT FIRST

Add your own products first. Frunio Plus starts with 3 days free; the app shows the price before you pay and reminds you the day before the trial ends. If you stop, your data stays and you can still read it.
